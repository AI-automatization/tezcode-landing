## 1. Y Combinator Startup Directory (FREE) — TOP PRIORITY

**URL:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies

**Nima uchun bizning eng yaxshi manbaimiz:**
- Har YC company founder publik, contact email yoki Twitter ochiq
- W26 batch = 196 ta company (Demo Day mart 2026), ~64% B2B, ~22 solo founder
- Founder'lar har doim dev hiring + AI tooling-savvy = Tezcode fit ideal
- Tashkent dev rate ($30-50/h) US/EU rate ($150-300/h) ga nisbatan 3-5x arzon
- YC alumni network kuchli — bitta muvaffaqiyat case keyingi 5-10 lead beradi (referral chain)

**Filterlash strategiyasi:**
1. Batch: W26 + S25 + W25 (last 18 oy)
2. Status: Active
3. Tag: "B2B" + "SaaS" yoki "Developer Tools" yoki "Healthcare" yoki "Fintech"
4. Team size: 1-30 (kichik jamoa, dev burst kerakli)
5. Manual filter: founder Twitter/email ochiq (har sahifada bor)

**Tipik query yo'l:**
```
https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch=Winter+2026&industry=B2B
https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/health-tech
https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/saas
```

**Hit rate estimate:** 5-10% reply (warm — founder ko'rsa response beradi)

## 2. Product Hunt (FREE) — recent makers (oxirgi 7-30 kun)

**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/daily/{year}/{month}/{day}/all

**Nima uchun ishlamoqda:**
- Founder shu hafta launch qildi, energiya yuqori, post-launch growth dard
- "Maker of the Week" har biri ochiq Twitter/email
- Solo founder yoki 2-3 odam team → custom plugin / integration sotuv mumkin
- Comments ochiq — biz qiymat beruvchi feedback bera olamiz (warm-up tactic)

**Filterlash strategiyasi:**
1. Last 7-30 kun monthly leaderboard skim
2. Top 5-10 har kunlik
3. "SaaS", "Developer Tools", "Productivity" tag prioritize
4. Maker profil → Twitter/email → website → bilingki kompaniya yangimi?

**Yangi launch + low team size sweet spot.** Bizning template T3 (Custom) yoki T5 (YC dual offer).

**Hit rate estimate:** 3-7% reply (cold lekin warm context, "saw your launch")

## 3. Hacker News "Show HN" + "Who's hiring" (FREE)

**Show HN URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/show

**Who's hiring URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring (oyiga 1 marta)

**Nima uchun ishlamoqda:**
- Show HN = founder o'zi qo'l bilan post qiladi, kontakt har postning oxirida
- Who's hiring = oyiga 1000+ ish e'lon, ko'pchiligi remote-OK + tech stack ochiq → biz dev sotamiz
- HN community AI-savvy, Claude/Cursor ishlatadi (bizning skill match)
- HNHIRING.com (3rd party) — filter by location, tech stack, remote

**Filterlash strategiyasi:**
1. Show HN last 14 kun → manual scan: B2B SaaS, dev tools, AI infra
2. Who's hiring → React / Node / TypeScript + "remote" filter → 50-100 lead/month
3. Avoid: pure data science / ML research lab (Python heavy, biz fit emas)

**Hit rate estimate:** 2-5% reply (cold, lekin tech stack match strong)

3. Founders profile → Twitter / website / open contact

**Hit rate estimate:** 4-8% reply (peer-to-peer warm)

## 7. GitHub Trending + Stars (FREE)

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/sales/

**Nima uchun ishlamoqda:**

- "Recently hired" / "Just got funded" filter — buying trigger

**Filterlash strategiyasi (Sales Navigator):**

- Company headcount: 11-200
- Geography: UAE, UK, DE, US, KSA
- Industry: Computer Software, Hospital & Health Care, Marketing Services

**Free version trick:**

**Hit rate estimate:** 5-10% connect accept, 2-4% reply (Sales Nav 2x boost)

**Cost:** $99/oy 1 user (Bekzod uchun), $159/oy team. Tezcode Custom 1 deal $5-15K → pay-back 1 mijoz.

## 10. Apollo.io (PAID $99-149/oy, hozir hold) — last resort

**URL:** https://apollo.io

**Nima uchun hozir HOLD:**
- $99-149/oy/user → Tezcode hozircha bepul/arzon manbalarda yetadi
- Apollo lead quality 60-70% (60% data accuracy, 40% bounce/no-reply)
- LinkedIn Sales Nav + warmly.ai + manual research = Apollo equivalent

**Qachon yoqish kerak:**
- Manual research 50 lead/hafta limit hit qilgansiz
- Pipeline conversion 2%+ ko'rsatkichni hit qildingiz (lead quality vs quantity)
- Email infrastructure ready (warmup, multi-domain, deliverability monitor)

**Apollo filterlar (kelajak uchun):**
```
Job titles: CEO OR CTO OR "VP Engineering" OR "Head of Engineering" OR Founder
Company size: 11-200 employees
Industries: Information Technology and Services, Computer Software, 
            Internet, Marketing and Advertising, Hospital & Health Care
Locations: United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, United Kingdom, 
           Germany, United States
Technologies used: React, Node.js, Next.js, AWS, Stripe
Funding stage: Seed, Series A, Series B
Recent activity: "Posted in last 90 days"
```

**Boolean job title query (Apollo Pro):**
```
("CEO" OR "Chief Executive") AND NOT ("Recruiter" OR "HR" OR "Investment")
```

**Hit rate estimate:** 1-3% reply (volume game, quality o'rta)

**Cost vs benefit:** $1500/yil. 1 deal $5K break-even. Pipeline mature bo'lganda yoqing.

## 13. Etik chegaralar (Rule 22 compliance)

- **FAQAT publik kontakt:** company website "contact" sahifa email, founder Twitter ochiq, LinkedIn publik profile
- **Private email YO'Q:** Apollo "verified email" — bu publik lekin scraped, GDPR EU'da risk
- **GDPR compliance (EU):** har email'da unsubscribe link + Tezcode address footer
- **CAN-SPAM (US):** physical address Tezcode Tashkent footer, sender authentic, no misleading subject
- **Volume limit:** birinchi 30 kun max 30 email/day/sender → IP warmup, gradual scale
- **Domain rotation:** tezcode.ai → secondary tezcode.io / tezcode.dev (kelajak Q2 2026)
