## 2. Top 5 Industry Vertical (sanoat tarmoqlari) + tipik Pain Points

### 2.1 E-commerce / D2C brands ($5K-50K LTV range)
- **Tipik mijoz:** Shopify Plus brand $5-25M ARR (yillik daromad), 5-50 xodim
- **Pain points:**
  - Shopify Plus migration project ($100-300K, 12-20 hafta) — agency wait list 3-6 oy
  - Custom Shopify app (subscription, bundle, loyalty) — in-house dev yo'q
  - Headless storefront (Hydrogen/Next.js) — Storyblok/Contentful integratsiya
  - Multi-store expansion (9+ store), Plus narxi $300/oy/extra store ko'paymoqda
  - Conversion rate optimization (CRO) — frontend dev qiymati past
- **Bizning hujum yo'limiz:** "We built a Shopify Hydrogen storefront for client X in 3 weeks — let's show you" → portfolio + screencast
- **Manba:** Easyappsecom Shopify Plus Multi-Store Strategy 2026, BuddyMagazine top DTC agencies 2026

### 2.2 Marketing agency (rekloma agentligi) — Tezcode Teams sweet spot
- **Tipik mijoz:** 10-80 xodim agency London/NYC/Berlin/Sydney, founder + 2-3 partner
- **Pain points:**
  - **Burst capacity** (qisqa muddatli yuk) — yangi klient kelganda 2 hafta ichida frontend dev jamoasi kerak
  - Western dev rate yuqori ($100-150/h London, $150-300/h US)
  - Junior remote dev quality low (no English, no AI tooling)
  - Project deadline kechikishi → klient yo'qotish
  - Internal hiring vaqt yo'qotadi (3 oy interview, onboarding)
- **Bizning hujum yo'limiz:** "Tashkent senior, $30-50/h, EN fluent, AI-skilled, same-week start" + 1 trial day risk-free
- **Manba:** RemoteGrowthPartners 2026 offshore guide, Avenga nearshore Europe 2026

### 2.3 Healthcare / Clinic management software (sog'liqni saqlash)
- **Tipik mijoz:** clinic chain 3-20 branch (UAE/Saudi/Europe), or healthtech startup seed/Series A
- **Pain points:**
  - Clinics miss 30-40% inbound calls (Knit Health, PraxiPal data)
  - Manual EHR (electronic health record — elektron tibbiy karta) data entry — 2 extra hours/day per doctor
  - Insurance claim processing slow (manual workflow)
  - Patient appointment booking — SMS/WhatsApp/web integration kerak
  - HIPAA/GDPR compliance (yuridik talab) — local dev bilmaydi
- **Bizning hujum yo'limiz:** "Carevy reference (Tezcode portfolio) — clinic management 3 oyda" + AI receptionist demo
- **Manba:** Y Combinator Healthcare IT 2026 (Klarify, PraxiPal/Luna), Fierce Healthcare May 2026 Enzo Health $20M

### 2.4 EdTech (ta'lim texnologiyasi) — emerging SMB platforms
- **Tipik mijoz:** UK/EU edtech seed/Series A, 5-40 xodim, B2B school SaaS or upskilling platform
- **Pain points:**
  - LMS (learning management system — o'quv boshqaruv tizimi) MVP build kerak — 4-6 oy in-house
  - Video processing (recording, transcript, AI quiz generation) — Python/FFmpeg expertise yo'q
  - Multi-tenant arxitektura (har school alohida) — backend complexity
  - i18n (internationalization — ko'p-tilli) majburiy
- **Bizning hujum yo'limiz:** "i18n UZ/RU/EN bazis, Tezcode internal OpenClaw flagship product, har kun ishlatamiz"
- **Manba:** Beauhurst Top 100 UK Edtech 2026, Seedtable 44 best UK Edtech 2026

### 2.5 FinTech (moliyaviy texnologiya) — early-stage MENA + EU
- **Tipik mijoz:** seed/Series A fintech UAE/Saudi/UK/Germany, 8-30 xodim
- **Pain points:**
  - PSD2 / Open Banking API integration (Plaid, TrueLayer, Tink)
  - KYC (know-your-customer — mijozni tanish) / AML automation
  - Sharia-compliant product (UAE/Saudi) — yuridik + tech kombinatsiya
  - Real-time transaction monitoring — Node.js/Python heavy
  - Mobile-first (React Native / Flutter)
- **Bizning hujum yo'limiz:** "Tashkent fintech ecosystem 5+ year, $15B Tamara MENA → biz region tushinamiz"
- **Manba:** EconomyMiddleEast Q1 2026 MENA funding $625.8M, Saudi Vision 2030 health+fintech

## 4. Top 10 Job Title + Decision-Making Power

Order by decision speed (qaror tezligi) — Tezcode pipeline uchun:

1. **Founder / CEO (solo founder yoki co-founder)** — *Decision power: HIGH, cycle: 3-14 days*. Sweet spot — 10-50 employee SaaS, Y Combinator alumni. Personalized email yuqori ochilish.
2. **CTO** — *Power: HIGH, cycle: 7-21 days*. Tech vetting strict (code sample, GitHub, Carevy demo). Bizning sweet spot — "AI-skilled, Claude/Cursor/Copilot daily".
3. **Head of Engineering** — *Power: MEDIUM-HIGH, cycle: 14-30 days*. Capacity planning (3-6 oy oldindan). Burst capacity uchun ideal.
4. **VP Engineering** — *Power: HIGH (50-200 employee company)*. Procurement + legal + onboarding 30-60 day cycle. Higher LTV, longer cycle.
5. **Engineering Manager** — *Power: MEDIUM, cycle: 21-45 days*. CTO approval kerak, lekin pipeline boshlovchi.
6. **Founder / Operator (non-tech)** — *Power: HIGH, cycle: 7-30 days*. Custom project sotuvi sweet spot — "Sen tushuntir, men qilaman" approach.
7. **COO** — *Power: HIGH (operations-heavy company)*. Internal tool kerak (dashboard, automation) — Custom project sweet spot.
8. **Product Manager** — *Power: LOW-MEDIUM*. Champion role — qaror egasi emas, lekin biz uchun yo'l ochuvchi. CTO/VP Eng tomonga eskalatsiya.
9. **CMO / Head of Marketing** (agency tomonida) — *Power: MEDIUM, agency client work*. Agency capacity bottleneck egasi.
10. **Founder of Marketing Agency** — *Power: HIGH*. 10-80 employee agency, burst capacity asosiy pain.

**Avoid (qochish kerak):**
- Recruiter / HR — bizning xizmat staffing-only emas, ular long-term contractor istaydi
- Junior PM — qaror egasi emas, vaqt yo'qoyadi
- "Director of Innovation" / "Head of Digital Transformation" — corporate buyurtmachi, vaqt 6+ oy

## 5. Buying Triggers (qachon ular Tezcode kabi xizmatga muhtoj?)

Trigger = "shu sodir bo'lgan kunda email yetib borsa, javob darajasi 5x oshadi"

### A. Funding triggers (moliyaviy raund)
- **Just raised seed / pre-seed** ($500K-3M) — engineering kapasiteti 0→3, scale yo'q, founder MVP qurish dard. **Custom project sweet spot.**
- **Just raised Series A** ($5-20M) — tezda jamoa kerak (3 oy 5→20 odam), in-house hire slow. **Teams sweet spot.**
- **Series B+ but capital efficient** — burn rate kuzatuv, offshore arzon. **Teams sweet spot.**

### B. Hiring signals (ish e'lonlari)
- LinkedIn yoki careers page'da "React engineer, $80-120K, London/remote" 3+ position 30 kun ochiq — internal hire muvaffaqiyatsiz, offshore kerak.
- "Looking for fractional CTO" — Custom project sweet spot.

### C. Product launch / pivot
- Product Hunt #1 yoki Top 5 (last 7 days) — founder energiya, post-launch growth, infrastructure scale dard.
- "Show HN" yangi product (last 14 days) — early adopter, dev capacity past.
- Y Combinator W26 / S26 batch — Demo Day pre/post, Tashkent dev fit.

### D. Tech stack signal (BuiltWith / Wappalyzer)
- React + Node + AWS + Stripe — Tezcode stack match 100%.
- Shopify Plus migration in progress (mavjud Shopify standard, traffic 50K+/oy) — D2C custom dev sweet spot.

### E. Industry trigger
- Healthcare clinic — new branch opening, EHR vendor switch.
- E-commerce — Black Friday / Ramadan / Singles Day 2 oy oldin (Q3 dev push).
- EdTech — new academic year prep (Q1/Q3).

### F. Media trigger
- TechCrunch / Crunchbase News funding announcement — same day email (subject: "Saw your TechCrunch piece on [round]").
- Founder podcast appearance — personalize: "Listened to your conversation with [host], the part about [topic] resonated".

## 6. Anti-ICP (kim mijoz EMAS — vaqt yo'qotmaslik uchun)

### Hech qachon target qilmang:
1. **Enterprise / Fortune 500 procurement** — 9 oy procurement cycle, RFP (request for proposal — taklif so'rovi), legal review. Tezcode hozir kichkina, fit emas.
2. **Crypto / NFT / Web3 speculative project** — payment risk, sanksiya / OFAC (US Treasury sanksiyalar idorasi) compliance murakkab.
3. **Gambling / adult content** — visa / payment processor restriction, reputation risk.
4. **Government contract** — Tashkent local government chiqib boshqa Davlat contract'iga kirish murakkab (procurement law).
5. **Solo founder no-revenue + no-funding** — to'lash imkoni yo'q (ya'ni *runway* = budjet muddati yo'q).
6. **"AI startup" pre-product + pre-fund** — kichkina budget, scope creep, payment risk.
7. **Russian / Belarus entity post-2022** — sanksiya restriction, banking issues.
8. **Indian dev shop / Pakistani agency** — biz competitor, ular emas mijoz.
9. **Bangalore / Manila based startup** — local dev ecosystem mature, Tashkent advantage past.
10. **Healthcare US (HIPAA strict) on cold outreach** — compliance + BAA (Business Associate Agreement — biznes hamkor shartnomasi) huquqiy talab katta, faqat warm intro orqali.

### Yellow flag (ehtiyot bo'ling, lekin to'liq rad emas):
- LinkedIn "founder & CEO" lekin company website yo'q → fake, skip
- Stripe/PayPal disabled country (Iran, Cuba, North Korea, etc.) → to'lov imkonsiz
- "I'll pay in tokens" — to'lash USD bo'lishi shart
- "Equity instead of cash" — early-stage iqtisodiyat, faqat strategic case'da
