## SEO state (as of 2026-08)

- **Healthy:** hreflang (5 locales + x-default), sitemap-index, robots.txt (AI
  bots allowed), canonical, llms.txt (with per-service prices).
- **Schema:** Organization+LocalBusiness, Service+Offer, FAQPage, HowTo,
  BreadcrumbList, Review+AggregateRating — on 20+ pages.
- **Known weakness:** backlinks concentrate on the homepage (was ~94% of 63
  links), few point to service pages → brand ranks, service intent lags. Fix
  via internal linking (done: RelatedServicesSection) + service-page backlinks
  from own products (wewatch.uz etc.) + directories, over 6–18 months.
